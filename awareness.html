SUBJECT ⚠️ Action Required: Protect Your GBP Account from Phishing Attacks

Dear GBP Community,

Phishing attacks targeting post-secondary institutions are at an all-time high. Attackers are sending fake emails that appear to come from college administration, IT Support, or Brightspace — designed to steal your credentials or trick you into authorizing fraudulent transactions.

🎣 Real example of a phishing subject line circulating on campus:
"Urgent: Your GBP account will be suspended — verify now"
This is a fake. GBP IT will never threaten account suspension via email without prior notice.

How to spot a phishing email — check for these 3 red flags:

1. Suspicious sender
Check the full email address — not just the display name. Attackers use domains like gbp-support@gmail.com.
2. Urgency & pressure
Legitimate IT requests never demand immediate action or threaten penalties within hours.
3. Suspicious links
Hover over links before clicking. Real GBP links end in georgebrown.ca — nothing else.
What to do if you receive a suspicious email:
  1. Do not click any links or open attachments.
  2. Click the Report Phishing button in Outlook — it's in your toolbar.
  3. Or forward it directly to security@georgebrown.ca.

As part of the PhishGuard program, you may receive simulated phishing emails in the coming weeks. These are safe training exercises — if you click one, you'll see a short tip explaining what to look for. No one will be penalized.

Want to test your phishing awareness right now? Take the PhishGuard quiz — it takes about 5 minutes.

Phishing attacks succeed because of habit. PhishGuard builds a better one.
