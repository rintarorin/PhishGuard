About PhishGuard

PhishGuard is a 12-month Security Education, Training & Awareness (SETA) program developed by Group 2 as part of COMP 4066 at George Brown Polytechnic.

The program targets administrative staff, faculty, and students — the three primary risk groups identified in the GBP threat assessment — with tailored training content for each.

Key KPIs tracked: phishing simulation click-through rate, quiz completion rate, average quiz score, and security incident reports.
